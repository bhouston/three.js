Light probe capture regressions

Two real 2 × 2 × 2 probe bakes, at a translated position. Direct lights are removed for these views: all illumination comes from the baked probes.

The reference uses the same capture cameras, atlas and SH projection, rendering one face at a time as CubeCamera does. The two images should match.

Union uses hardware clipping when supported; fragment union uses nine planes to exercise its shader fallback. Alpha-to-coverage applies to the multisampled final views; probe captures use the production single-sample atlas.
