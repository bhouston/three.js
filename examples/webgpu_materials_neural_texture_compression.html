three.jswebgpu - materials - neural texture compression
Loads a pre-trained .ntc (Neural Texture Compression) asset - a small multiresolution latent grid + MLP decoder, NVIDIA NTC style - and evaluates it directly on a real THREE.MeshPhysicalNodeMaterial (NTCLoader.js / NTCMLPNode.js). The decoder's weights live in a real fp16 storage buffer wherever the backend supports it (native WebGPU + the shader-f16 feature), falling back to fp32 otherwise.
